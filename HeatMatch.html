This is the standalone, single-file build of FT Heat Map — it opens straight from your hard drive, no install or server. The geographic basemap needs internet for its map tiles; everything else (scores, tables, charts) works fully offline.

What this is

FT Heat Map joins heat supply (Barcelona's crematoria) to plausible heat demand (pools, hospitals, hotels, laundries, district-network nodes) and scores every source→sink pairing with a Heat Score (0–100). The pun is deliberate: a hot pair is both physically recoverable and politically plausible.

How the Heat Score works

technicalFit = 0.40·proximity + 0.27·demandFit + 0.20·profileFit + 0.13·infrastructure
socialPoliticalFit = governance × sensitivityMultiplier
heatScore = min( cap(sensitivity), 0.60·technicalFit + 0.40·socialPoliticalFit )

The social cap is the key idea. A high-sensitivity sink (schools, care homes, children's hospitals, food production) is capped at 60 and can never be "hot", no matter how good the engineering — because community acceptance, not engineering, is what usually kills these projects (the Redditch precedent). Open the Map or Opportunities tab and click any item to see this play out.
